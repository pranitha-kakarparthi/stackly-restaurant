Yes! You can pause or cancel your pass directly through your Stackly Patron Dashboard with zero penalties or hidden cancellation fees.

Absolutely. All tasting passes are fully portable across all Stackly dining rooms, lounges, and affiliated pop-up dinners globally.

Yes, our digital gift passes come in an elegant presentation email with a personalized note and customized chef welcome greeting.
