Our Heritage & Passion

Crafting authentic Asian culinary masterpieces through 16+ years of mastery, organic farm-to-wok integrity, and deep reverence for flavor harmony.

Since 2010

A Journey of Passion, Fire and Flavor

Stackly Asian Cuisine began with a single vision: to revive time-honored Asian culinary traditions in their purest form. Frustrated by commercialized shortcuts and artificial flavorings, Executive Chef Qun Lin set out to construct a kitchen governed solely by authenticity.

From our hand-hammered carbon-steel woks roaring over high heat to our 24-hour slow-simmered bone broths, every dish tells the story of generations of culinary artistry.

Farm to Table

Where Every Flavor Begins: The Soil

We believe that extraordinary cuisine is impossible without extraordinary ingredients. We contract directly with regional growers in Tamil Nadu and New York state for pesticide-free scallions, ginger root, baby bok choy, and heirloom wild mushrooms.

Our seafood is sustainably line-caught daily, and our poultry is grain-fed and free-range. When you dine at Stackly, you taste the untamed purity of nature.

The Craft

Three Sacred Kitchen Rituals

From high-heat wok tossing to 48-hour bone broths, each plate honors centuries of culinary discipline refined with modern gastronomic precision.

🔥

800°F Wok Hei Mastery

Our cast-iron woks roar at extreme searing temperatures, sealing caramelization and imparting that elusive smoky breath of the wok in under 45 seconds.

🍲

48-Hour Simmered Broths

Marrow bones from grass-fed Wagyu and charred kombu are extracted slowly over two days, yielding a collagen-rich broth that coats the palate with pure umami.

🥟

Hand-Folded Precision

Each dumpling wrapper is rolled paper-thin by hand every dawn, filled with hand-chopped tiger prawns and folded in 18 meticulous artisan pleats.
