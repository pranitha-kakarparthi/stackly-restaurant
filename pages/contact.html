Quick Answers

Dining & Reservation FAQs

Do you accommodate severe peanut, gluten, or shellfish allergies?

Yes! Our kitchen maintains strict segregated prep zones and dedicated woks for gluten-free and allergen-free preparations. Please notify our hosts when reserving.

Is parking provided at the Salem flagship location?

Complimentary valet parking is available directly at the MMR Complex main forecourt for all dining patrons and banquet guests.

What is the cancellation policy for table reservations?

Standard table bookings may be modified or cancelled up to 2 hours prior without fee. For Chef Tasting Omakase tables, we appreciate a 24-hour advance notice.
