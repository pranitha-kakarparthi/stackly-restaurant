Wood-Fired Discipline

800°F Passion in Every Seared Grain

Wok Hei—the sacred "breath of the wok"—is the smoky, caramelized aroma born when high-heat carbon steel seals in moisture, crispness, and flavor in under 90 seconds.

🔥

Hand-Hammered Carbon Steel

Every wok is seasoned daily with cold-pressed peanut oil over open flame, forging an organic non-stick patina that imparts depth to every stir-fry.

⚡

Flash-Sear Precision

Operating at blistering 800°F temperatures, our chefs flash-sear meats and baby vegetables, locking in vibrant natural sugars and vitamins.

🌿

Zero Chemical MSG

We extract authentic umami through dried Japanese kombu, Yercaud shiitake mushroom reductions, and unrefined natural sea salts.

🍲

24-Hour Master Broth Pot

Our signature bone broth pot never sleeps—simmered gently with roasted beef marrow, whole ginger roots, star anise, and aged citrus peel.

Guest Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about dining reservations, allergen safety, private events, and our culinary philosophy.

Do you accommodate severe celiac, gluten-free, or shellfish allergies? +

Yes! We take food allergies with utmost medical seriousness. Our kitchen maintains dedicated sanitized woks, separate frying oil baths, and gluten-free tamari soy sauces. Please notify your server or note it during reservation booking.

How far in advance should I book for weekend dining? +

We strongly recommend reserving 48 hours in advance for Friday and Saturday dinner services. However, our cocktail lounge and walk-in waitlist always accept spontaneous parties on a first-come basis.

Do you host private celebrations, corporate banquets, or full buyouts? +

Yes! Our Bamboo VIP Suite accommodates up to 20 guests for private milestone dinners, while full restaurant buyouts accommodate up to 140 guests with custom multi-course menus and dedicated sommelier service.

What is your corkage policy for vintage wines? +

Our corkage fee is $25 per 750ml bottle (up to two bottles per dining table). The corkage fee is gladly waived for each bottle purchased directly from our curated cellar list.

Is parking and valet service provided at the Salem flagship? +

Yes! We offer complimentary valet parking at our main front porte-cochère for all dining guests, along with on-site secure parking and EV charging stations.
